Mento Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026

Mento is an anonymous support chat for adults (18+). You are never asked for your name, a photo or a phone number. Everyone appears under a made-up name. We do not show ads, we do not sell data, and we do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

What we collect

  • Date of birth, once at signup, to check that you are 18 or over. It is never shown to anyone.
  • Email address, only if you choose to add one, to help you get back into your account. It is never shown to mentors or other members.
  • Your chats. Messages are stored on our own servers, encrypted in our database, so both people can read the conversation. No outside chat company stores them.
  • Things you choose to save: journal entries, the optional mentorship form (exam stage, attempts, marks and a note), your community choice, your companion and its name, and an optional recovery code (we keep only a one-way hash of it).
  • Account and device identifiers: a random account ID, a one-way hash of a random install label the app makes (not a hardware or advertising ID), sign-in sessions, and a notification token so we can send you notifications.
  • Safety records: when you agreed to the house rules, and any suspension or ban.
  • Mentors only: the languages they speak, their exam history as checked by our team, and, if they send one, a proof document. That document is deleted as soon as our team decides on it, or after 30 days if nobody has.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, phone number or advertising ID.

Who can see what

  • The person you talk with sees only your made-up name and, if you set them, your companion and colour. Never your age, email or identity. If you are both a member and a mentor, your two sides have different names.
  • Crisis safety. Every message is checked automatically on our server for signs of crisis before it is stored or delivered. If one is found, you are shown helplines and the conversation is flagged for our safety team. A filter also hides likely personal details, such as phone numbers, in messages.
  • Our safety team can read a conversation only while reviewing a crisis flag or a report about that exact conversation, with a recorded reason. Every such view is logged.
  • Notifications show the other person's made-up name and what happened. They show the words of a message only if you turn that on yourself. Notifications are delivered through Google's push service.
  • We do not send your chats or journal to any AI service, analytics service or advertiser.

Deleting your data

  • Clean Wipe (in a chat's options) ends the chat and deletes its messages from our servers, for both people.
  • Start fresh (in Profile) deletes your account: your details, journal, mentorship form, saved mentors and every conversation with its messages.
  • We keep a few safety records after deletion: crisis flags (the kind of signal, not your words), reports made by or about you with the reporter's own words, and our staff audit log. The link to your account is removed.
  • If you are also an active mentor, ask us to close your mentor side first; then Start fresh deletes everything.
  • No app? See how to delete your account.

Backups

Our database is backed up every night, encrypted, and kept for 30 days. An encrypted copy is also taken just before each update. Chat messages are never included in any backup, so a deleted chat cannot come back. Other deleted data can remain in a backup until that backup is removed.

Age

Mento is only for people aged 18 and over. Age is checked on our server at signup.

If you are in crisis

Mento is peer support, not therapy or emergency help. In India, call Tele-MANAS, free and 24/7: 14416 or 1800-89-14416. In an emergency, call 112.

Changes

If what we collect or who can see it changes, we update this page and its date first.

Contact
