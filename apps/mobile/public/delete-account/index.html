Delete your Mento account

Mento, by the Mento team · updated 16 November 2026

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Mento and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Start fresh.
  3. Tap Delete everything and start fresh.

Your account is deleted at once. If something goes wrong, the app says so and asks you to try again; it never says data was deleted when it was not.

To delete just one conversation, open it, open its options and choose Clean Wipe.

Without the app

Email geekspacetech02@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Mento account”. Mento has no names or phone numbers, so tell us the made-up name your account uses and, if you added one, the email on your account. We delete the account within 7 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account: made-up name, date of birth, email, companion, community choice and recovery code.
  • Every conversation and all of its messages, for both people.
  • Your journal, mentorship form and marks, saved mentors, requests and notification settings.

What we keep

  • Safety records: crisis flags (the kind of signal, not your words), reports made by or about you with the reporter's own words, and our staff audit log. The link to your account is removed.
  • Encrypted backups never contain chat messages. Other deleted data stays in a backup until that backup is removed; nightly backups are removed after 30 days.

If you are also an active mentor, email us first so we can close your mentor side, then delete as above.
