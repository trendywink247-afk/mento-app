# Self-assessment suite and Community: how I would approach them

Status: thinking only, not built. Both stay deferred (`DECISIONS.md` §D.8 and `CLAUDE.md` scope); this is for the founder to react to. 2026-11-08.

## Self-assessment suite (Mirror / Knowledge Assessment / Preparation Challenges)

**What it is for.** Mento's promise is a human to talk to. An assessment only earns its place if it makes the next conversation better or the member's own week clearer. Exam-prep quizzes (knowledge tests) are a different product with a different audience; they should not live inside the support app.

**The version I would build first: a private check-in, not a test.**
- A two-minute weekly "How are you carrying this?" with four or five sliders (sleep, focus, mood, pressure, support), asked in plain words.
- **No score is shown.** The member sees their own trend line in the Journal, next to their entries. No grades, no streaks, no badges (T&S #5: no dependency engineering).
- **Stored on the phone by default.** The member can choose to share a one-card summary with a mentor at the start of a chat. The mentor sees a sentence, never raw answers.
- **Never a gate** and never a diagnosis. Copy says what it is: a mirror, not a measure.
- **A safety hook:** a very low run of answers shows the helpline card and offers to talk to a mentor now. It does not auto-alert anyone.

**What stays out.** Subject-knowledge quizzes, mock tests and rankings (a coaching product, other incentives, other liabilities), anything that looks like a clinical screen (PHQ-9, GAD-7) without a clinician behind it, and any shared leaderboard.

**Risks.** A score creates pressure in an app people open when they are already under pressure. Health-adjacent answers raise the privacy bar (`PRIVACY.md`, Data safety form). Content needs a subject-expert reviewer. Build only after own chat is verified and Module A is solid.

## Community

**Today.** "Community" is a lens: the Path picks UPSC, NEET, JEE and so on as a soft matching preference (`DECISIONS` §N.1). That is the right foundation.

**Why not an open forum.** Fifteen mentors cannot moderate one. Group spaces leak anonymity (writing style, details), need the crisis scan on every post, attract harassment and spam, and turn mentors into moderators.

**A staged path that earns each step:**
1. **Curated, read-only (cheap, safe).** "People at this stage often ask" cards written or reviewed by mentors, per exam and stage. Already half-built on the Path.
2. **Moderated anonymous Q&A.** A member asks one question to a niche; two or three mentors answer in public; the question is anonymised and scanned first. No free-form replies, no direct messages.
3. **Small time-boxed cohorts.** Six to eight people at the same stage, four weeks, one mentor host, ends on schedule. Warmer than a forum and moderation scales with hosts.
4. **Open spaces: last, or never.**

**Gate for each step.** Moderation and reporting tools, the crisis scan on group posts, a staffing plan (who reads reports at 2 a.m.), and updated privacy copy.

**Recommendation.** Finish the Path and the exam options (this week), then step 1. Decide on step 2 only after real mentors say they have the time.
