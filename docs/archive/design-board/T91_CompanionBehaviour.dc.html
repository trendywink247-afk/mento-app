The screen is your companion's furniture

Alive does not mean moving about. Every time you arrive at a screen, your own companion is already there, clinging to a different place on it, and it stays there, breathing. It is always the animal the member chose, never a fixed cat. Open T07 in Play mode, tap the tabs, and switch whose companion it is.

Six ways to be on the furniture

On topSits on the top edge of a card, a row or the tab bar. Every screen's home is one of these.
DanglingSits on an edge, hind legs over the side. Only where no words are under it.
HangingHolds on under a card's bottom edge by its front paws, looking at you.
PeekingHalf hidden behind an edge. Only the head and the front paws show above it.
LeaningStands beside the write bar or a key and leans in, curious.
NappingAsleep along an edge. Only late at night, or after a long quiet spell.

What makes it cling. The ring marks the edge the companion is pinned to. Every pose is drawn with a fixed contact point (the paws, the seat line, the bottom of a peek), and the app puts that exact point on the edge of the furniture. Nothing floats.

When it goes still

A struggling person is never teased On these screens there is no randomness. The companion is on its home perch in one quiet comfort pose (a plain sit for animals without one), and it does not react to taps.

Reduced motion. It still turns up somewhere new on each arrival, because placing is not motion. It does not breathe and it does not react. Quiet Pause and Away Mask. The companion is hidden.

How it is built

One companion layer sits above the whole app. Each screen tells it where its perches are, measured on the real layout.On arrival the layer picks a perch, places the companion while it is still hidden, and lets it fade in with the screen. Its position is never animated.A small table per animal says which poses exist. That table alone decides which perches it can get, so new art widens an animal's range with no new code.Reduced motion: it still lands somewhere new (placing is not motion), but it does not breathe or react. One still flag from crisis, error and limit screens sends it home.No new library. No 3D engine. No sound. T07 is playable. Four tabs and one deeper page. Tap a tab, even the one you are on, to arrive again.
