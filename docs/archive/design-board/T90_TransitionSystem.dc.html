One way of moving, for the new design

Today the app has five unrelated hand-overs. This replaces them with one family, designed on the screens you picked (the letter, the alive chat with its header card, Path 2, Journal 3, My Chats 2 with In touch), not on the old ones. The phones to the right are playable: open one in Play mode and tap the real button.

The six principles

1One continuous skyThe background never cuts. It only shifts a little, position and warmth, from screen to screen.
2The companion travelsYour animal never vanishes and reappears. It moves to its next perch, arriving just before the content.
3Things come from where you touchedThe tapped key, row or card is where the next surface grows from, and where it returns on back.
4Leave fast, arrive slowOld content is gone in 200 ms before new text is readable. Never two headlines at once.
5Direction means somethingForward rises, back is the exact reverse, tabs move sideways in tab order, sheets rise while the page settles back.
6Calm, alwaysTransform and opacity only. 200 / 350 / 500 / 700 ms. No bounce. Reduced motion is a 150 ms fade. Errors stay still.

Against Mento's motion rules

Fits the rules as writtenTransform and opacity only. Token durations. No overshoot. Reduced motion completes every flow as a fade. No audio. Crisis card, message limit and errors never animate.
Needs your ruling: three moversThe rule says at most three things moving at once. A staggered group (four rows arriving 80 ms apart) is counted here as ONE mover. T05 is the busiest: avatar, name, surface.
Web is the test surface, not the targetRoute changes on web are a cut, so shared elements land by fade there. The full choreography is for iOS and Android. The end state is identical, so the e2e specs stay valid.
What this costsOne origin store, one companion overlay, one sky context, one depth value, a tab scene interpolator. No new library, no 3D engine, nothing that touches the chat kit.
