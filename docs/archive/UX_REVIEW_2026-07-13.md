## Verdict

The app **meets the international B2C bar** on its core loop. The onboarding journey is
genuinely self-explanatory — no manual needed: one CTA on landing, trust-first copy at
the age gate, an honestly skippable email, a companion pick that instantly re-tints the
whole sky (the single most delightful beat), and a matched chat in seconds. Empty states
all have art, safety copy is everywhere it should be, and the anonymity promise is
restated at each moment it matters. The gaps below are polish and copy, not structure.

## Recommendations for founder review (design calls — not implemented)

1. **Chat header truncates the persona name** — "Serene Moun…" at 390px. The name IS the
   relationship; the "Connected" pill + subtitle crowd it out.
   *Recommend:* shrink the pill to a status dot, or drop the "Here to listen and
   support" subtitle to free a line. Small change, high visibility.
2. **Reflection copy contradicts the no-ratings promise.** The screen says "How would
   you rate this conversation?" and "Your feedback helps us create a better Mento" —
   but DECISIONS frames this as a *private self-reflection* (drained ↔ energized), and
   the Mentors screen explicitly promises "No star ratings here."
   *Recommend copy:* "How do you feel right now?" + keep the privacy note. No layout
   change needed. (Philosophically load-bearing; copy-only fix.)
3. **Companion step: the "Can't decide? / Surprise Me" card sits half-hidden** behind
   the sticky Continue button on first viewport. *Recommend:* tighten vertical spacing
   so it clears, or move Surprise Me up beside the section header.
4. **Reflection slider dots are small** for one-handed thumbs. *Recommend:* keep the
   visual size, enlarge the touch target (hitSlop).
5. **Chats tab shows search + filter chips with one conversation** — noise before it's
   useful. *Recommend:* reveal search/filters only once ~5 conversations exist.
6. **Coffee: disabled payment methods only reveal "coming soon" on tap.**
   *Recommend:* a small "Soon" tag on each method row so no tap is wasted.
7. **Gender filter (PRD §6.3) is not in the UI.** The API already returns listener
   gender. This is a sensitive product call (some users prefer a same-gender listener;
   it also fragments a small listener pool). *Founder decision:* include in v1 or
   drop from PRD.
8. **Listener console at desktop width** is the mobile layout stretched to ~1280px.
   Fine for v1 ops; *recommend* a max-width (~720px) container for readability.
